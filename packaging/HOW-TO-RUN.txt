SECRET SANTA - HOW TO RUN
=========================

Double-click "secret-santa" (Windows: "secret-santa.exe").
A small window opens and the Secret Santa form opens in your web browser.
Keep the small window open while you use the form. Close it when you're done.

Your participants and settings are remembered in your browser for next time.
Nothing is sent anywhere except the Secret Santa emails themselves.


FIRST-TIME WARNINGS (normal for free apps that aren't code-signed)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Windows:  "Windows protected your PC" -> click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

Mac:      "cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software"
          -> click Done, then open System Settings -> Privacy & Security,
             scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to secret-santa.
          (Older macOS: right-click the file -> Open -> Open.)

Linux:    If double-clicking doesn't run it, open a terminal in this folder and run:
              chmod +x secret-santa && ./secret-santa


SENDING EMAIL
-------------
Open "Email settings" on the form. For Gmail:
  SMTP server: smtp.gmail.com     port: 587
  IMAP server: imap.gmail.com     (used to delete the sent copies)
  Sent folder: [Gmail]/Sent Mail  Trash folder: [Gmail]/Trash
  Password:    a Gmail App Password (https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords),
               not your normal password.

Click "Check email settings" to test them. It signs in but sends nothing.
Always try "Test mode: show pairings" first. It sends nothing.

If you get an email error:
  - "closed the connection" -> check the server name and port. Use 587 or 465.
    Antivirus with email scanning (Avast, AVG, ESET...) can also cause it.
    Turn that scanning off for a moment and try again.
  - "rejected the username or password" -> use an app password, not your
    normal one. Outlook.com/Hotmail can't be used to send from apps.
  - The app says whether any emails went out before the error, and to whom.


COMMAND-LINE VERSION (optional)
-------------------------------
  secret-santa cli                         type participants in
  secret-santa cli people.csv --test       preview pairings from a CSV
  secret-santa cli people.csv              draw and send
